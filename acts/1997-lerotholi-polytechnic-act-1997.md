LEROTHOLI POLYTECHNIC ACT, 1997 ACT NO. 1 OF 1998 An act to establish the Lerotholi Polytechnic and to provide for incidental matters. Enacted by the Parliament of Lesotho.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY

Short title and commencement

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Lerotholi Polytechnic Act 1997 and shall come into operation on the date to be appointed by the Minister by notice in the Gazette.

Interpretation

### Section 2

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires“Academic Board” means the Academic Board of the Polytechnic established under section 17; “academic staff” means members of staff of the Polytechnic appointed on academic terms of service and includes the Director and the Deputy Directors; “administrative staff’ means members of staff of the Polytechnic who have not been appointed on academic terms of service; “Alumni” means former students of Lerotholi Technical School, Lerotholi Artisan Training Centre, Lerotholi Technical Institute, Technician Training School and Commercial Training Institute; “Council” means the Governing Council of the Polytechnic established under section 7; “Director” means the Director of the Polytechnic; “Minister” means the Minister responsible for education; “Ordinances” means the Ordinances of the Polytechnic made under this Act; “Polytechnic” means the Lerotholi Polytechnic established under section 3; ‘‘special resolution’ means a resolution passed at the meeting of the Council by a majority of not less than three-fourths of those present and voting; “Statutes” means the Statutes of the Polytechnic made under the authority of this Act; “student” means a person admitted and registered into an academic programme of the Polytechnic whether fulltime or part-time;

“University” means any University to which the Polytechnic is affiliated.

Disqualification from office

### Section 8

(1) The following people are not qualified to be members of the Council:

(a) an unrehabilitated insolvent except with the leave of the High Court;

(b) any person who at any time has been convicted of high treason, sedition, or any crime or offence involving dishonesty or violence to the person of another;

(c) any person suffering from mental incapacity within the meaning of the Mental Health Law, 19641

(2) An appointed member shall vacate his office as an appointed member, if he,

(a) resigns office by writing to the Minister;

(b) becomes an ex-officio member of the Council;

(c) is absent, without leave granted by the Council, from three successive meetings of the Council of which due notice has been given; or

(d) ceases to hold the office or qualification in respect of which the person was appointed a member of the Council.

Casual appointment

### Section 9

If a vacancy occurs in the office of an appointed member of the Council, the Minister shall appoint a person qualified to fill the vacancy for the remainder of the term of the appointed member and the person appointed to fill the casual vacancy may at the end of the term be reappointed if still eligible.

Delegation by the Council

### Section 11

(1) The Council may, in relation to any matter, activity or function, delegate by an ordinary resolution any of its powers under this Act except the power of delegation and its powers in relation to the annual adoption of a budget and the approval of the proposed distribution of funds referred to in section 26 of this Act, to the Director or to any committee appointed by the Council or to any other officer or officers of the Polytechnic.

(2) A delegation under this section may be varied or revoked by an ordinary resolution of the Council, and no delegation shall prevent the exercise, discharge or performance of any power, authority, function or duty by the Council.

Director

### Section 12

(1) There shall be a Director who shall be the chief academic and administrative officer of the Polytechnic.

(2) The Director shall, subject to the provisions of this Act, Statutes and Ordinances, control and direct the activities of the Polytechnic, maintain discipline and control and direct the staff of the Polytechnic in the implementation of those activities.

(3) The Director shall, subject to the approval of the Minister, be appointed by the Council.

(4) Notwithstanding sub-section (3), the first Director shall be appointed by the Minister.

(5) The Director shall be appointed for a period of five years and is eligible for reappointment. The terms and conditions of appointment shall be specified in the instrument of appointment.

(6) The office of the Director shall become vacant (a) if the Director resigns;

(b) if the Council, with the consent of the Minister, removes the Director from office for inability to perform his duties or for misbehaviour; or

(c) at the expiry of his term of office. Deputy Directors

### Section 13

(1) There shall be a Deputy Director (Academic Affairs) and a Deputy Director (Administration) who shall have such powers and shall discharge such duties and functions as specified in the Statutes or which are assigned to them by the Director.

(2) The Council shall, in consultation with the Director, appoint the Deputy Directors.

(3) Notwithstanding subsection (2), the first Deputy Directors shall be appointed by the Minister.

(4) The Deputy Directors shall hold office for a term of four years and are eligible for reappointment.

Registrar

### Section 14

(2) The Registrar shall, in addition to his functions and duties act as Secretary to the Council and the Academic Board.

Functions of the Academic Board

### Section 18

(1) The Academic Board shall assist the Council and the Director in the planning, coordination and development of the academic activities of the Polytechnic.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), the Academic Board has the following powers:

(a) to prepare annual, long-term and a five year rolling academic development plan of the Polytechnic;

(b) to design, develop and implement appropriate programmes of study;

(c) to make recommendations to the Council for the establishment of appropriate structures for the implementation of programmes of study;

(d) to direct and regulate the research carried out under the auspices of the Polytechnic;

(e) to make rules for the conduct of examinations and for the evaluation of academic programmes;

(f) to appoint external examiners and academic consultants or advisers;

(g) to make rules for the award of fellowships; scholarships, bursaries, prizes or other awards;

(h) to make general arrangements for tutorial or other academic counselling;

(i) to regulate and control the admission of persons to courses of study in the Polytechnic;

(j) to formulate and promulgate a Code of Conduct to be observed by the students of the Polytechnic and to regulate the discipline of the students;

(k) to recommend to the University the award of certificates; and

(1) to consider the budgets of departments and schools of the Polytechnic in respect of teaching and research for recommendation to the Council.

(3) The Academic Board may establish standing or special committees to assist it in the performance of its duties.

(4) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Academic Board may regulate its own procedure.

Employment of staff

### Section 20

(1) The Council may, subject to the approval of the Minister, employ such staff as it thinks appropriate.

(2)

Subject to subsection (3), the terms and conditions of service of the staff of the Polytechnic shall be determined by the Council after consultation with the Academic Board.

(3) The remuneration and allowances payable to the staff of the Polytechnic shall be subject to the approval of the Minister.

(4) A member of staff of the Polytechnic may not be dismissed unless the person has been provided with reasons for the proposed dismissal and has been given an opportunity to reply.

Existing staff

### Section 21

(1) Any person who, immediately before the coming into operation of this Act, was employed full-time at the Lerotholi Technical Institute, Technician Training School or the Commercial Training Institute, as the case may be, may within the period of six months of the coming into operation of this Act, if he so wishes, elect to terminate his employment with the Public Service and thereafter serve under the Polytechnic.

(2)

A person employed by the Lerotholi Technical Institute, Technician Training School or the Commercial Training Institute who, on or after the commencement of this Act, elects to serve under the Polytechnic shall,

(a) be regarded as an employee of the Polytechnic; and

(b)

subject to Pensions Proclamation 19642, be paid any pension and or gratuity accruing at the time of his election to serve under the Polytechnic.

Retirement of Staff

### Section 22

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act as specified in the Statutes, Academic Staff shall retire from the Polytechnic on attaining the age of sixty-five and administrative staff shall retire from the Polytechnic on attaining the age of sixty.

(2) An academic staff member may, on or at any time after attaining the age of fifty-five years and subject to three months notice being given, retire or be required to retire.

(3) An administrative staff member may, on or at any time after attaining the age of fifty years and subject to three months notice being given, retire or be required to retire.

(4) If, in the opinion of the Council it is in the public interest to retain an academic or administrative staff member in his post beyond the age of sixty-five years or sixty years respectively, that member of staff may, if willing, be retained for such a period as the Council may determine.

## PART III — FINANCE

Funds

### Section 23

The funds available for the purpose of enabling the Polytechnic to perform its functions under this Act consist of (a) all moneys or property that may from time to time(i) be appropriated by Parliament;

(ii) be donated, lent or granted to the Polytechnic by any Government or by a person or an international organisation;

(b) all moneys borrowed by the Polytechnic under this Act;

(c) fees paid by students of the Polytechnic;

(d) all moneys derived from the disposal of, or dealing with, movable or immovable property vested in or acquired by the Polytechnic, or derived as income from investment by the Polytechnic; and

(e) all moneys received by the Polytechnic for goods or services provided under the authority of this Act.

Application of revenue by the Polytechnic

### Section 24

(1) All fees and all other moneys received by the Council shall be applied by the Council solely for the purposes of the Polytechnic.

(2) For the purposes of this section, the application of moneys by the Council for the purpose of (a)

enabling a member of staff of the Polytechnic to pursue study. or research at any University or elsewhere;

(b) the advancement of learning generally; or

(c) assisting any affiliation, association or connection with another institution, shall be deemed to be an application of those moneys for the purposes of the Polytechnic.

Borrowing power

### Section 25

Subject to the Loans (Statutory Bodies) Act, 19753, the Polytechnic may with the consent of the Minister, raise or borrow money with or without interest and on such terms as may be agreed upon between the lender and the Polytechnic.

Budget

### Section 26

(1) The Council shall in each financial year adopt for the next financial year, a budget for all funds of the Polytechnic other than those specified in subsection (2) and shall control the expenditure of the Polytechnic so that it conforms as nearly as practicable to the approved budget.

(2)

The Council shall review annually funds available to the Polytechnic by way of bequest, donation, or special grant, and must subject to the terms of any trust and before any expenditure is made, approve the proposed disposition of these funds.

(3) The entire budget (including the subvention, required from the public funds) shall be submitted to the Minister for the Minister’s approval.

Accounts and audit

### Section 27

(1) The Council shall correctly keep accounting and associated records that correctly record and explain all transactions.

(2)

The accounts and records of financial transactions of the Polytechnic shall be audited each year in accordance with the Audit Act, 19734.

(3)

The Auditor-General, or any officer authorised by him, has a right of access to the accounting and associated records of the Polytechnic and is entitled to require from any officer or employee of the Polytechnic information necessary for the purposes of the audit, and that officer or employee must comply with any reasonable requirement.

(3) The Auditor-General shall report to the Council the results of the audit and, if he deems necessary, shall make recommendations to the Council in regard to the conduct of the financial transactions of the Polytechnic and the manner of operating and maintaining the accounts. The Auditor-General shall certify whether or not the statements of accounts submitted exhibit a true and fair view of the financial transactions of the Polytechnic for the period to which they relate.

(5)

The Council shall, as soon a practicable after the thirty first day of March, and in any event, not later than one month after the receipt of the certificate of the Auditor-General referred to in subsection (4) prepare and give to the Minister statements of accounts in the form approved by the Auditor-General and Auditor’s report.

(e) the appointment, quorum and duties of committees of the Council and Academic Board;

(i) the granting, withholding and cancellation of certificates, diplomas and degrees;

Every Statute made by the Council shall be sealed with the common seal of the Polytechnic and transmitted to the Minister for approval.

(4)

Statutes shall have no force and effect unless and until they have been approved by the Minister.

(5)

The Council may make Ordinances for the carrying into effect the provisions of this Act.

Students’ Union

### Section 29

(1) There is established a union to be known as the Lerotholi Polytechnic Students’ Union, which shall have such powers and be subject to such obligations as may be prescribed by or under the Statutes.

(2) All students of the Polytechnic shall be members of the Students Union.

(3) The Students’ Union shall conduct its affairs under the supervision and direction of the Director.

(4) The constitution of the Students’ Union and all amendments shall be submitted to the Council through the Academic Board and shall have no force or effect unless approved by the Council.

Annual report

### Section 30

The Council shall, not later than six months after the end of each academic year, prepare a report of the proceedings and activities of the Polytechnic during the preceding academic year and submit it to the Minister.

Special Resolution Meeting

### Section 31

Notice of a meeting requiring a special resolution shall be given at least a month before the date of such a meeting.

Property and assets

### Section 32

(1) Any property or asset which immediately before the coming into operation of this Act was vested in the Government of Lesotho for the purposes of the Lerotholi Technical Institute, Technician Training School or Commercial Training Institute, shall, from the coming into operation of this Act, vest in the Polytechnic subject to any conditions, liabilities or obligations upon or under which it was vested immediately prior to the coming into

operation of the Act.

(2) Where, immediately before the coming into operation of this Act, any person holds any property or assets in trust of the Government of Lesotho for the purposes of the Lerotholi Technical Institute, Technician Training School or the Commercial Training Institute, that person shall, from the coming into operation of this Act, hold such property or assets of the like trust for the Polytechnic.

Rights, liabilities and obligations

### Section 33

All rights, liabilities and obligations of the Government of Lesotho in respect of the Lerotholi Technical Institute, Technician Training School or the Commercial Training Institute shall be, from the coming into operation of this Act, rights, liabilities and obligations of the Polytechnic and enforceable by or against the Polytechnic accordingly.

Legal proceedings pending

### Section 34

Legal proceedings pending immediately before the coming into operation of this Act by or against the Government in relation to the Lerotholi Technical Institute, Technician Training School or the Commercial Training Institute may be continued on or after the coming into operation of this Act by or against the Polytechnic as the party to the proceedings.

Regulations

### Section 35

The Minister may make Regulations not inconsistent with the Act, prescribing all matters which by this Act are required or permitted to be prescribed or which are necessary or convenient to be prescribed for carrying out or giving effect to this Act.
